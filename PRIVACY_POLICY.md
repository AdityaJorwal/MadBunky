# Privacy Policy for MadBunky Pro

**Last Updated: January 01, 2026**

MadBunky Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by MadBunky Pro.

This Privacy Policy applies to our website, and its associated subdomains (collectively, our "Service") alongside our application, MadBunky Pro. By accessing or using our Service, you signify that you have read, understood, and agree to our collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Location Data (Geofencing)
MadBunky Pro may request access to your device's location to enable the "College Geofence" feature. This feature automatically reminds you to mark attendance when you enter or leave your university campus.
-   **Usage**: Detailed location data is processed **locally on your device**. We do not transmit your location history to any external server.
-   **Storage**: Your defined "College Zone" coordinates are stored locally on your device.

### B. Network Information (WiFi Triggers)
To provide the "WiFi Attendance Trigger" feature, the app requires permission to access information about your current WiFi network (SSID/BSSID).
-   **Usage**: This is used solely to detect when you have connected to your designated university network to prompt attendance marking.
-   **Storage**: Trusted WiFi names are stored locally on your device.

### C. Attendance & Academic Data
The core function of the app is to track your academic attendance.
-   **Usage**: All subjects, schedules, and attendance records are stored locally.
-   **Storage**: Data is stored in a local database on your device. If you use the "Backup" feature, data is encrypted and saved to a file location of your choice (e.g., your Google Drive/iCloud), subject to those platforms' own privacy policies.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:
-   Provide, operate, and maintain our app.
-   Improve, personalize, and expand our app.
-   Send you push notifications regarding your attendance (e.g., "You have entered college", "Attend next class").

## 3. Third-Party Services

We may employ third-party companies and individuals due to the following reasons:
-   To facilitate our Service (e.g., Google ML Kit for timetable scanning).
-   To provide the Service on our behalf.

**Google ML Kit**: We use Google's on-device Machine Learning kit for text recognition (OCR) features. No image data is sent to Google's servers for this processing; it happens entirely on your device.

## 5. Children's Privacy
